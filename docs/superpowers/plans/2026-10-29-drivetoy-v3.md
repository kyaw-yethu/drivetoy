**Goal:** Build the DriveToy v3 dataset (`/root/projects/drive_v3`) with the eight fixes of `version-3-revision.md` and the decisions below.

**Architecture:** Every v3 change goes into the existing simulator, recipes, labels, exporter and Blender script in `/root/projects/drivetoy_v2`, switched on by `cfg["DT_V3"]`, the same way v2 used `DT_V2`. A new build script writes `drive_v3`, and a new check script measures each issue on the pilot before the full build.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 + NumPy (system `python3` on yt), Blender 4.2.3 Cycles at `/root/opt/blender/blender`.

**Spec:** `/root/projects/drivetoy_v2/version-3-revision.md`, Issues 1 to 8, plus the decisions below (agreed with the user on 2026-10-29). The scorer that reads the Issue 7 labels is described in `/root/projects/drivetoy_v2/task_encoder_eval.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Code lives in `/root/projects/drivetoy_v2` on yt-workspace; data goes to `/root/projects/drive_v3/<split>`; `drive_v2s/val` and `/root/projects/drivetoy_v2s_code` stay untouched.
- Every v3 behaviour runs only when `cfg.get("DT_V3")` is true, so `drive_v2s` val still re-simulates exactly from its stored `meta.json` cfg. v3 builds and tests use `dict(DRIVETOY_DEFAULT_CFG, **V3_OVERRIDES)`.
- `simulate(sim_seed, cfg, target)` must reproduce every v3 sample exactly; label code (`map`, `may_go`) draws no random numbers and changes no simulator state.
- Clip geometry is unchanged: $128 \times 128$ frames, $70^\circ$ camera, 5 Hz, 32 visible + 32 label-only frames, 16 futures forked at frame 31, split sizes and seed bases as in `build_drivetoy_v2.py`.
- Spec values: cars lead at 75 to 97% of the limit, trucks and buses at 65 to 85%, nobody below 60% without a reason; "follow" means a bumper gap of at most 2 s; oncoming gaps are 2 s plus an exponential tail with mean 4 to 8 s; the left-only lane carries about 25% of its approach; arrows stay on half the maps at 3 to 5 s; no tree crown between an approach and a sign or signal head from 10 to 60 m; `may_go` covers frames 31 to 51 in each of the 16 futures.
- Tests are plain scripts (`python3 tests/test_x.py`); pytest is not installed.
- Blender needs `LD_LIBRARY_PATH=/root/opt/cuda525`. Long jobs run as `nohup cmd > log 2>&1 < /dev/null & disown`.
- Rendered previews and GIFs get new file names (`*_v3.*`).

## Decisions

- **D1. Leads and overtakes.** The causes "lead vehicle" and "parked obstacle" and the overtake check (`overtake_problems`) bind within $\max(10\ \text{m},\ 2\ \text{s} \cdot v)$. An overtaking lead drives at 60 to 75% of the limit (above the spec's 60% floor, which stands in for the road's minimum speed), the ego at 90 to 97%, and the ego closes in to within 2 s before it pulls out.
- **D2. Per-future routes.** Each of the 16 futures draws its direction independently with equal odds over the moves its lane allows. No count is fixed and no minimum per direction is forced; a direction is missing in about 0.5% of three-way clips.
- **D3. `may_go` at a four-way stop.** True when no vehicle is inside the junction on a conflicting path and no vehicle waiting at another approach is moving. The scorer skips "didn't go" while a car that stopped before the plan still waits, so the rule never contradicts R4's arrival order.
- **D4. Open-route approach.** Until its route is drawn, the ego treats every move its lane allows as possible: it slows for the tightest one, and it enters only when the oncoming gap suffices for a left (if a left is allowed) and no pedestrian is on or entering any crosswalk those moves cross.
- **D5. School-zone triangle.** A filled blue triangle, 2.0 m wide and 4 m long, with its apex toward the approaching driver, starting 2 m inside the zone.
- **D6. Prompt starts (all splits).** Drivers go promptly once the way is clear: the delay before moving off is 0.2 to 0.6 s (`DT_START_DELAY`, v2: 0.3 to 1.5 s), and the full stop at a stop sign lasts 0.8 s (`R4_STOP_TIME`, v2: 1.0 s), still above R4's check of 4 stopped frames. At a stop sign the scorer's 1 s "didn't go" timer starts when the full stop ends.

## Review Focus

- **v2 drift:** a v3 code path that draws a random number or reads a new attribute in a v2 run would break `drive_v2s` re-simulation. Task 0's regression test runs after every task that touches `sim.py`, `scenarios.py` or `roads.py`.
- **Label side effects:** a `may_go` probe that touches the RNG or vehicle state would make futures differ from their labels. Task 7 adds a determinism test.
- **Late route draw:** a future that draws a turn for an ego close to its line at frame 31 could need braking beyond `DT_B_MAX` or break a rule. Task 5's integration test checks deceleration and `rules.check` over all futures.
- **Committed go into a closing gap:** a committed driver that ignores a car arriving inside the conflict area would collide. Task 2's test requires zero collisions across futures.
- **Shares after Issue 1 and D6:** normal-speed leaders and quicker starts change which targets are buildable, so "follow", "lead vehicle", "stop sign" and "crossing traffic" shares can collapse. Tasks 1 and 2 print the achieved labels for their targets, and the pilot reports all shares.
